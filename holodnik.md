# Holodnik

•servings• 4

## ingridients

- coocked beetroot - 250g
- kefir - 1l
- fresh cucumbers - 200 g
- eggs large - 4
- mineral water - around 100-200 g
- garlic - 1 glove
- dill - 25 g

## Technique

1. thinly slice cucumbers
2. boil eggs 
3. hashe garlic clove
4. grate beetroot (coarse)
5. mix beetroot with garlic and add kefir until soup consistency, and place in the fridge for 20 min
6. check the mixture and dissolve with mineral water if needed


## Plating
1. add cucumbers on the plate 
2. add beetroot mix
3. season with salt and black pepper
4. cut the egg in half and place on the top 
5. add thinly chopped dill
